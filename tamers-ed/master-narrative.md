# Digimon Tamers: E.D. — Master Narrative

**Status:** Work-in-progress master narrative. This document preserves the established five-arc structure, character relationships, renamed concepts, and locked story events. It also supplies connective scenes and a complete final conflict so the current material can be read as one continuous story. Individual attacks, several evolution lines, and episode-level divisions remain open for later development.

## Continuity

Digimon Tamers: E.D. takes place thirteen years after the defeat of the D-Reaper. The original television series remains the principal canon. Battle of Adventurers can remain part of the characters' history, while Runaway Locomon does not occur in this continuity because the events of E.D. contain the Tamers' first true reunion with their partners after the original separation.

Takato's discovery of a portal beneath Guilmon's former hiding place still occurs. The passage is a temporary residue left by the D-Reaper's removal and collapses before anyone can cross it. Hypnos spends the following years searching for another stable route, but Takato, Rika, Henry, and the others reach adulthood without seeing their partners again.

## Premise

Thirteen years after the Digimon were forced to leave the Real World, Guilmon, Renamon, Terriermon, and several of the other partners suddenly return. Their arrival is followed by attacks from rogue Digimon attempting to steal the data that allowed them to cross between worlds. The returning Tamers become involved with Naoki Sakurai and Kei Morita, two young adults whose partners, Salvodramon and Wulvermon, are connected to an older generation of artificial Digimon known as the T-X and D-X.

The T-X were numerous Tactical Extermination units. The D-X were a smaller group of Destruction Xtreme units created to confront threats the T-X could not destroy. When the ancient D-Reaper first became impossible to eliminate, both groups entered hibernation and used their own bodies and DigiCores to form a living containment system around it. The D-Reaper's removal at the end of the original series left the prison empty, damaged, and unable to understand that its purpose had ended. Thirteen years later, the failing system awakens its surviving weapons and begins collecting enough human and Digimon data to close itself around both worlds.

## History

### Before the Reunion

Following the D-Reaper crisis, Takato Matsuki, Rika Nonaka, Henry Wong, and the other Tamers returned to ordinary life without their partners. Takato continued helping at his family's bakery and filled several notebooks with designs for Digimon that he never attempted to create. He kept his original drawing of Guilmon, although the knowledge that his childhood imagination had produced a living being made it increasingly difficult for him to draw without considering what might happen if another design became real.

Henry remained in contact with Hypnos and eventually began working with the organization as an engineer specializing in unstable interfaces between the Real and Digital Worlds. His work began as an effort to recover Terriermon and became a broader attempt to prevent another emergency from reaching the city without warning. Rika competed for several years before withdrawing from the public Digimon card circuit. She kept her D-Arc and continued training alone, refusing to treat Renamon as a childhood phase even as the adults around her stopped asking when she expected her partner to return.

Rika also remained estranged from her father. He contacted her intermittently and made several promises to visit, but the relationship never became dependable. Rika learned to treat every proposed meeting as provisional and stopped admitting when she had waited for one. Rumiko and Seiko recognized the pattern but could do little when Rika insisted that the matter no longer affected her.

During the same thirteen years, Mao Kurose lived outside most official systems with Lilithmon. Mao had been ten years old and without stable housing when she found the injured Digimon in Shinjuku during the D-Reaper crisis. Lilithmon initially kept the child nearby because Mao could obtain food, information, electronics, and shelter without attracting the attention caused by a wounded Mega-level Digimon. Mao stayed because Lilithmon was dangerous enough to keep other dangers away.

When the Digimon were pulled back to the Digital World, Lilithmon resisted the forced return and began to come apart. Mao remained with her and attempted to hold her exposed data together. A damaged black-and-gold D-Arc formed between them as an emergency anchor, absorbing part of Lilithmon's collapsing data and preserving the remainder in the Real World. They spent the next thirteen years moving through temporary homes and forged identities while Mao learned to maintain the device with stolen components and improvised code.

## Characters

### Partners

#### Guilmon

Guilmon is Takato's partner and one of the three original Digimon who return at the beginning of E.D. He retains his trusting, curious, and highly physical personality from the original series. Thirteen years in the Digital World have given him more experience, but he has not become solemn or emotionally distant. He still responds to complicated relationships through immediate concrete categories, which is why he identifies Tsukikagemon and Nichirinmon as Renamon's sisters before the other characters finish analyzing their data.

His return reopens Takato's guilt over having designed him. Takato knows Guilmon became an autonomous person, but seeing him injured as an adult makes the childhood act of drawing him feel newly consequential. Guilmon rejects the idea that his creation makes every later choice Takato's responsibility. Takato supplied the first picture; Guilmon lived everything that followed.

Guilmon remains the most naturally destructive member of the Rookie group. His power is direct, instinctive, and difficult to separate from territorial behavior when a fusion magnifies it. His arc does not require him to become less dangerous. Takato must learn that trusting Guilmon includes trusting how he uses power, while Guilmon must accept commands and shared control without treating them as evidence that Takato fears him.

Guilmon participates in four DNA Digivolutions: Abyssdramon with Salvodramon, Komainudramon with Renamon, Jabberwockmon with Terriermon, and Cairndramon with Wulvermon. Together they expose four sides of him: uncontrolled appetite, sacred protection, childlike imagination, and the refusal to abandon someone who is lost.

#### Renamon

Renamon is Rika's partner and the third separated aspect of Taikyokumon. She begins E.D. with no knowledge of that origin. Her complete lack of inherited memory distinguishes her from Tsukikagemon and Nichirinmon, but it does not make her an incomplete copy. Renamon inherited Taikyokumon's adaptability, capacity for choice, and ability to construct a self through new experience. The sister who remembers nothing becomes the one most capable of deciding what their family will become.

She remains disciplined, observant, and sparing with visible affection. Her thirteen-year separation from Rika makes her more willing to state what she wants, particularly when Rika's fear of another abandonment becomes controlling. Renamon refuses to let Rika decide in advance that recovered memories or another family will take her away. She chooses Rika again while also accepting that Tsukikagemon and Nichirinmon can become her sisters.

Renamon's combat style emphasizes speed, redirection, ritual techniques, and precise judgment. Her fusions reveal qualities she rarely displays independently. Tangomon exposes a theatrical pride hidden beneath her restraint, Komainudramon gives her discipline a savage territorial body, Harequinmon turns her tactical intelligence mischievous, and Parhelimon allows her to share protection with Wulvermon without surrendering her decisions to him.

Renamon's bond with Wulvermon gradually becomes romantic. Their relationship is quiet and develops through repeated acts of attention. Wulvermon never asks her to choose between Rika and her sisters, while Renamon becomes one of the people capable of recognizing when his calm behavior conceals damage. His deletion during the failed Taikyokumon restoration leaves her unwilling to approach her sisters until Rika stops using the tragedy to justify permanent separation.

#### Terriermon

Terriermon is Henry's partner and the third returning member of the original central trio. His light manner and use of "Momentai" continue to balance Henry's habit of approaching danger through analysis and prevention. Terriermon understands more than his jokes suggest and frequently uses humor to stop Henry from turning care into surveillance.

During E.D., Henry's work with Hypnos places him in the position once held by the adults who monitored the Tamers. He can now identify almost every technical risk surrounding Terriermon and is tempted to remove those risks before Terriermon can choose how to face them. Terriermon's role is to remind him that safety is something they construct during danger, not a condition Henry can perfect in advance.

Terriermon contributes adaptability, compact power, unconventional problem-solving, and emotional elasticity to his DNA forms. His combinations are the most visually and mechanically varied in the matrix. Jabberwockmon treats reality like an unfinished story, Harequinmon controls information through deception, Barnstormon converts fear into aerial performance, and Breakwatermon turns "Momentai" into an enormous physical refuge.
