# Digimon Tamers: E.D. — Master Narrative

**Status:** Work-in-progress master narrative. This document preserves the established five-arc structure, character relationships, renamed concepts, and locked story events. It also supplies connective scenes and a complete final conflict so the current material can be read as one continuous story. Individual attacks, several evolution lines, and episode-level divisions remain open for later development.

## Continuity

Digimon Tamers: E.D. takes place thirteen years after the defeat of the D-Reaper. The original television series remains the principal canon. Battle of Adventurers can remain part of the characters' history, while Runaway Locomon does not occur in this continuity because the events of E.D. contain the Tamers' first true reunion with their partners after the original separation.

Takato's discovery of a portal beneath Guilmon's former hiding place still occurs. The passage is a temporary residue left by the D-Reaper's removal and collapses before anyone can cross it. Hypnos spends the following years searching for another stable route, but Takato, Rika, Henry, and the others reach adulthood without seeing their partners again.

## Premise

Thirteen years after the Digimon were forced to leave the Real World, Guilmon, Renamon, Terriermon, and several of the other partners suddenly return. Their arrival is followed by attacks from rogue Digimon attempting to steal the data that allowed them to cross between worlds. The returning Tamers become involved with Naoki Sakurai and Kei Morita, two young adults whose partners, Salvodramon and Wulvermon, are connected to an older generation of artificial Digimon known as the T-X and D-X.

The T-X were numerous Tactical Extermination units. The D-X were a smaller group of Destruction Xtreme units created to confront threats the T-X could not destroy. When the ancient D-Reaper first became impossible to eliminate, both groups entered hibernation and used their own bodies and DigiCores to form a living containment system around it. The D-Reaper's removal at the end of the original series left the prison empty, damaged, and unable to understand that its purpose had ended. Thirteen years later, the failing system awakens its surviving weapons and begins collecting enough human and Digimon data to close itself around both worlds.
