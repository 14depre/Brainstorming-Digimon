# Digimon Tamers: E.D. — Voice Guide (Draft)

> Working notes on how each returning character talks, based on their late-series voice in the original *Digimon Tamers* (after Rika and Renamon's arc, through the D-Reaper finale), then aged up thirteen years. Written from memory of the show, not from transcripts. Check against episodes before locking anything.
>
> **Reference version: English dub.** Notes below were checked against episode 39, "Song of Sakuyamon." Transcripts themselves are not stored in this repo; only voice observations are.

## How to use this

- **Late-series voice** = how they sound at the end of the original show
- **E.D. voice** = how thirteen years should change them without erasing them
- **Never write them like** = the trap to avoid

## The Original Trio

### Takato
- **Late-series voice:** earnest and emotional. Says what he feels the moment he feels it. Stumbles over words when he's flustered, apologizes a lot, and leads from the heart rather than strategy. After Megidramon and Jeri, there's guilt underneath everything.
- **E.D. voice:** steadier and quieter, but the earnestness never left. He thinks before speaking now, mostly because he's afraid of what his imagination can do. When he finally cuts loose (drawing again, defending Guilmon), the old kid comes back all at once.
- **Never write him like:** a confident action-hero leader or someone sarcastic.

### Guilmon
- **Late-series voice:** childlike, literal and food-driven. Simple sentences, sniffing things out, total trust. His world is Takato, bread and play.
- **E.D. voice:** more experienced but *not* more serious. He still sorts the world into simple, concrete categories (friend, food, family, danger), which is why he names relationships before anyone else. His simplicity often lands as accidental wisdom.
- **Never write him like:** a wise mentor, brooding, or using complex vocabulary.

### Rika
- **Late-series voice:** blunt, sharp and sarcastic, with short clipped sentences and teasing nicknames for the others. She hides tenderness behind gruffness, but by the end she openly fights *for* people, not just to win.
- **E.D. voice:** still blunt, now with adult control. She rarely says what she's afraid of; it comes out as planning, restricting and pre-deciding. When she's truly moved, she goes silent and *acts* (crossing the room, moving her bedding to the window).
- **Never write her like:** cold for no reason, or openly gushing about her feelings.

### Renamon
- **Late-series voice:** composed, formal and economical. Every word is deliberate. Calm even in crisis. By the end, her loyalty to Rika is absolute and quietly gentle.
- **E.D. voice:** the same restraint, with one change: she now *says what she wants* when it matters. Her humor is dry and deadpan ("It seems so." / "Intervention is unnecessary.").
- **Never write her like:** chatty, flustered in a cartoon way, or using slang.

### Henry
- **Late-series voice:** calm, reasonable and analytical. The responsible older brother who hates unnecessary fighting and tries to talk things through.
- **E.D. voice:** a professional engineer's precision. He explains, quantifies and plans, and his care comes out as risk assessment. Under stress he gets *more* technical, not louder.
- **Never write him like:** hot-headed or careless about danger.

### Terriermon
- **Late-series voice:** cheeky, snarky and relaxed. "Momentai." Teases Henry constantly and complains about being treated like a toy, but understands far more than he lets on.
- **E.D. voice:** the jokes are now a deliberate tool to stop Henry from spiraling. He knows exactly when to undercut the tension and when "Momentai" needs to mean something real.
- **Never write him like:** mean-spirited or genuinely careless.

## Returning Cast

### Impmon / Beelzemon
- **Late-series voice:** street-tough, defensive and proud, insulting people as a reflex. Post-redemption he's quieter, carrying real remorse, especially toward Jeri.
- **E.D. voice:** a little gruff, wiser and less defensive. He's the one who knows redemption is a daily choice and says so bluntly.

### Jeri
- **Late-series voice:** cheerful on the surface early, hollowed out by grief after Leomon, then slowly recovering by the finale.
- **E.D. voice:** gentle and honest. She names hard truths softly and refuses to rush other people's healing because nobody rushed hers.

### Calumon
- **Voice:** pure childlike joy, sing-song and simple. Unchanged by time, which is part of his charm.

### Kazu and Guardromon
- **Kazu:** loud, boastful and impulsive, but loyal. As an adult he's still the loudest person in the room.
- **Guardromon:** polite, formal and earnest in a robotic way. Devoted to Kazu.

### Kenta and MarineAngemon
- **Kenta:** nervous, nerdy and supportive. Adult Kenta is still the anxious one who shows up anyway.
- **MarineAngemon:** tiny, cute and mostly wordless. Communicates through sounds and hearts.

### Yamaki
- **Voice:** clipped, controlled and cold on the surface. Few words. His softened late-series self is still terse, but fair.

### Seiko and Rumiko
- **Seiko:** calm, wise and gentle. The person who explains feelings without making a big deal of it.
- **Rumiko:** warm, bubbly and dramatic. Can't keep her composure for more than a few seconds.

## New Cast (from the E.D. draft)

- **Naoki:** cautious, polite and deadpan. The straight man. Corrects Salvodramon's version of events with dry precision.
- **Salvodramon:** cocky, loud and theatrical. Claims every accident as a plan. Goes quiet only when something actually scares him.
- **Kei:** relaxed, cool and witty, a card shark who talks in odds and gambling metaphors ("The odds became offensive"). He notices everything and comments on little of it.
- **Wulvermon:** stoic, kind and minimal. Speaks rarely and plainly. Never complains about his own pain.
- **Mao:** sharp, fast-talking and defensive. Assumes every question is an accusation and trades sarcasm for trust.
- **Lilithmon:** regal, theatrical and cutting toward everyone except Mao, then dramatic and tearful in private. Speaks like royalty even in a trench coat.
- **Tsukikagemon:** soft-spoken, precise and patient, with persuasion through omission.
- **Nichirinmon:** loud, warm and direct. Talks with her whole body.
- **ChaosGallantmon:** formal and absolute. Speaks in designations, functions and verdicts.
- **Sanguinarymon:** hostile, cutting and bitter. Refuses every label offered to him.
- **Chaosmon:** guarded and argumentative. Disputes every word anyone uses to describe him.
- **Anglemon:** mechanical, command-language phrasing ("PASSAGE. AUTHORIZED."), with his real voice breaking through in fragments.

## Observed in Episode 39, "Song of Sakuyamon"

### Rika
- Public voice is all jabs: one-word insults, rhetorical put-downs, backhanded praise ("for a boy"). She refuses help out loud ("Do I look like I need rescuing?").
- **Private voice exists only with Renamon.** She asks real questions (can destiny change? will Jeri be okay?) in short, almost childlike lines.
- She decides through action, then announces it in one line. Her biggest moment is a choice, not a speech: stay and fight *with* Renamon.
- Cares about people without saying so to their face. Her worry for Jeri comes out only to Renamon.

### Renamon
- Speaks in short, complete, philosophical sentences about **destiny and choice**: the past can't change, but you choose what it makes of you, and everyone is responsible for their own destiny.
- Her calm makes her rare emotional breaks huge. When she begs Rika to leave, it's the most desperate she ever sounds.
- **E.D. connection:** her late-series philosophy *is* the trait she inherited from Taikyokumon. The sister built around choice has been preaching choice since the original series.

### Sakuyamon (note for the "I" vs "we" rule)
- Sakuyamon speaks as "I" and Rika describes the merge as "I am Sakuyamon and she is me." That's a willing union.
- **Rule refinement:** "I" isn't wrong in itself. The failed Taikyokumon's "I" is wrong because it erases consent and treats the sisters as parts. Sakuyamon's "I" is two people who chose to be one voice.

### Takato
- Earnest reactions and quick protective outbursts ("Don't say that!"). Notices when someone is hurting and goes straight to them.
- Has a light, dorky sense of humor in tense moments (lava lamp, "Or not.").

### Guilmon
- Food and simple sensory comparisons (cotton candy cloud, home is "where all the bread is").
- Treats obvious things as obvious and gently calls others silly for missing them.

### Henry
- Analytical and pragmatic. Under pressure he proposes the limited, achievable goal instead of the heroic one.
- Dry humor when impressed by someone else's chaos.

### Terriermon
- Jokes that puncture the mood at the worst possible time, and "Moumantai" used to reassure others, not just himself.

### Kazu, Kenta, Ryo, Calumon
- **Kazu:** "Dude," weird similes ("crazier than a soup sandwich"), roasts Kenta constantly.
- **Kenta:** catastrophizes ("we're doomed"), gets motion sick, is loyal anyway.
- **Ryo:** cocky charm, teases Rika, quietly competent.
- **Calumon:** talks himself into bravery out loud, then thinks about cream puffs.
