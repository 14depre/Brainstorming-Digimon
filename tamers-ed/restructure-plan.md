# Digimon Tamers: E.D. — Restructure Plan

The current master narrative is a **reference draft**. It covers the whole plot but moves at speedrun pace. This file tracks what needs to be addressed before the story is rebuilt from zero with room to breathe.

## 1. Format decisions (need answers first)

- [ ] **Target length.** The original Tamers ran 51 episodes. Is E.D. a full 50-episode season, a shorter 26-episode season, or episode-free prose chapters?
- [ ] **Arc count.** Keep five arcs, or let the restructure decide how many arcs the story needs?
- [ ] **Filler philosophy.** Which kinds of breather episodes do we want? (slice of life, character pairings, comedy, side missions, support cast spotlights)
- [ ] **Point of view.** Ensemble like the original, or does one character anchor each arc?

## 2. Pacing problems in the current draft

- [ ] **The reunion is over in a few paragraphs.** Thirteen years of waiting deserves at least an episode or two per original trio member before the plot accelerates.
- [ ] **Too many introductions per arc.** The Awakening Arc alone introduces ChaosGallantmon, Sanguinarymon, the Raydramon reveal, both sisters, the Taikyokumon lore, Chaosmon, four DNA forms, the prison's expansion and Anglemon's return.
- [ ] **DNA forms debut in batches.** Komainudramon, Jabberwockmon and Moondramon share one paragraph. Each fusion should get its own episode with its own relationship lesson.
- [ ] **New Tamers get partnered too fast.** Naoki and Kei both earn their D-Arcs within one arc. Their lives before the partnership barely appear.
- [ ] **The support cast returns in one paragraph.** Kazu, Kenta, Suzie, Ryo, Jeri, Ai and Mako each need at least a beat.
- [ ] **Lore arrives through convenient files.** Mao recovers a file, the records describe the prison, the record shows Taikyokumon. Spread reveals out and let characters discover them through action.
- [ ] **Relationships are summarized instead of shown.** Renamon and Wulvermon, Rika and Mao, Naoki and Kei, Impmon and Jeri, and Lilithmon letting go all need actual scenes.

## 5. Structural proposals

### Chaosmon as the early false threat (proposed)

Move Chaosmon ahead of the T-X and D-X so the first act has a tangible antagonist before the faceless prison is revealed.

**Why it works**
- He is already written as a false villain who acts on an understandable but wrong conclusion.
- His unstable body senses the prison forcing data together before anyone else can, so he becomes an early warning nobody believes.
- Jeri's thread with him gets a full season to develop instead of one encounter.
- It replaces the generic rogue hunters as the first big fight.

**What needs solving**
- [ ] He is the "Fusion Killer," but DNA Digivolution doesn't exist yet in act one. Options:
  - He targets the partner-Tamer links themselves at first, believing the reunions feed the prison.
  - Abyssdramon's debut happens *during* a Chaosmon fight, and his reaction to it turns him into the Fusion Killer.
- [ ] How does he relate to the bribed hunters? Ally, rival, or a third side?
- [ ] When does the audience learn he's not the real threat?

### Other ideas to consider

- [ ] Give each DNA fusion its own episode built around the pair's relationship flaw (the first-attempt failure pattern already works).
- [ ] Stagger the D-X: ChaosGallantmon and Sanguinarymon currently wake almost back to back.
- [ ] Introduce the sisters through rumors or sightings before the big fight.
- [ ] Plant Anglemon earlier, glimpsed or heard through his lure, so the dungeon reveal pays off.
- [ ] Decide where the eight prison units (Suturemon, Klaxonmon, Rampartmon, Hushmon, Vellumon, Scaffoldmon, Gauzemon, Scabbardramon) appear and whether they are T-X mechanisms.

## 7. Decisions made during restructuring

- **More arcs.** Five is too few. Working list is ~12 arcs (Reunion, New Faces, Fusion Killer, Old Friends, The Prison Below, The Black Knight, Raydramon, Sun and Moon, Brother Conflict, Deletion, The Seal, Final Battle). Arc count is still open.
- **Chaosmon is the early false threat**, appearing before the T-X and D-X.
- **Mao is a full Tamer**, not a subplot. She needs an on-screen moment where she chooses the team.
- **Demon Lord arc (idea).** One or two Demon Lords (e.g. Barbamon offering to heal her, Daemon as muscle) try to bring Lilithmon back. The offer to restore her would remove her need for Mao's anchor. It forces the "pride or Mao?" answer. Place it after Mao joins the team. Possible Beelzemon angle.
- **Lilithmon's refusal to retreat** stays ambiguous early (pride, Mao, or both) and becomes a late emotional reveal.
- **Abyssdramon comes after the Sanguinarymon reveal.** Its gore look is Salvodramon's buried D-X architecture surfacing, combined with Takato's Megidramon fear. Naming it becomes Naoki affirming Salvodramon's identity. It is no longer the first DNA form.
- **First DNA form:** an original-trio pair first (proposed: Komainudramon), with Tangomon as the first cross-generation fusion right after.
- [ ] **Chaosmon's Fusion Killer trigger** moves to the first DNA; Abyssdramon later "proves him right."
- [ ] **Red fusions:** possibly only after the reveal, once Salvodramon stops suppressing that data.
- **Project title: Digimon Tamers: Passage Authorized.** Replaces "E.D." The original 2008 title, *Digimon Tamers: Edición Dorada*, stays as trivia and as the repo codename.
- **Bible format:** a live Claude Doc bible with tabs (like the Danza Macabra setup).
