# Digimon Tamers: E.D. — Support Cast Plan (Proposal)

> **Proposal, not canon.** How to give the original secondary Tamers real roles without letting them crowd the main cast.

## The rule

Every returning character gets three things:

1. **A theme link:** they reflect one of E.D.'s core questions (purpose after the mission, creation, love without ownership, choice).
2. **A plot function:** something the story can't do without them.
3. **One spotlight:** at least one episode, or a strong scene inside a bigger episode, that's theirs.

E.D.'s core questions, for reference:
- What do you do when the thing you were built for is over? (Anglemon, ChaosGallantmon, Sanguinarymon)
- Does creating someone make you responsible for every choice they make? (Takato, Salvodramon)
- How do you love someone without owning them? (Rika, Lilithmon, the sisters)
- Is redemption one act or a daily choice? (Sanguinarymon, Beelzemon)

## Tiers

| Tier | Characters | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **1: Real arcs** | Jeri, Impmon/Beelzemon, Ryo | Threads that run across multiple arcs |
| **2: Spotlights** | Kazu, Kenta, Suzie, Calumon | One spotlight episode each plus a recurring job |
| **3: Function and cameos** | Ai and Mako, Yamaki, Riley and Tally, Janyu and the Monster Makers, Kai, Takato's parents | Key scenes only |

## Tier 1

### Jeri
- **Theme:** grief without ownership. She lost Leomon and was absorbed by the D-Reaper.
- **Unique asset:** **she is the only human who has been inside the D-Reaper.** The prison is the D-Reaper's cage, still running. Her trauma becomes expertise: she recognizes the feeling of a mindless system wearing a familiar face.
- **Plot function:** the Chaosmon thread. She's the one who sees the third person in the middle of his triple core. In the final arc, she's the first to recognize the illusions, because the D-Reaper trapped her in one once.
- **Jeri and Chaosmon get genuinely close.** No official "partner" label, since Chaosmon disputes every label anyone gives him.
  - **Shared wound:** both were used as vessels. The D-Reaper used Jeri as a container and a hostage; Chaosmon is a container for two other DigiCores. They understand each other without explaining it.
  - **Jeri kept her old D-Arc** for thirteen years, the way Rika kept hers. It starts reacting to Chaosmon. The question is whether it's responding to the BanchoLeomon data or to Chaosmon himself. The device can't tell. Jeri decides it's him.
  - **Visual payoff:** the D-Arc only fully wakes once his triple core stabilizes, glowing the pink of his center core, not the gold of BanchoLeomon's.
  - **Beelzemon triangle:** Chaosmon starts out protective of Jeri around Beelzemon.
  - **Decision: they become partners.** Jeri gets the happy ending the original series struggled to give her.
- **Jeri's E.D. ground rules**
  - **No new suffering spiral.** She is not kidnapped, possessed or used as bait again. The original already put her through her mother's death, Leomon's death, the D-Reaper and more.
  - **She has done the work.** Thirteen years of healing (including therapy) are part of who she is. Her trauma is history and insight, not her whole personality.
  - **Her strength is seeing clearly.** When she recognizes the prison's illusions, it reads as earned wisdom, not a relapse.
  - **The partnership isn't a replacement for Leomon.** It's earned through the Chaosmon storyline and happens on Chaosmon's terms, because the choice has to be his.
  - **Timing (proposed):** during his recovery after the final battle, when his triple core stabilizes and her D-Arc wakes pink.

### Impmon / Beelzemon
- **Theme:** redemption as a daily choice. He killed Leomon. That never stops being true.
- **The big hook: the Jeri / Beelzemon / Chaosmon triangle.** Chaosmon carries data that resembles BanchoLeomon. Beelzemon has to stand in front of someone wearing an echo of the Digimon he killed, next to the girl he hurt. Does Chaosmon hate him? Not care? Recognize him? This is the strongest secondary storyline in the series.
- **Ai and Mako:** now around nineteen. Impmon went to them first. He used to resent being their toy; now he's fiercely protective and doesn't want them anywhere near a fight. That's Lilithmon's possessiveness arc in a lighter key, and the two of them can call each other out on it.
- **Demon Lord arc tie-in:** when Demon Lords come for Lilithmon, how do they see Beelzemon? A pretender who borrowed the title? A traitor? Someone they want back too? Lilithmon and Beelzemon as **"the two Demon Lords who babysit humans"** is both comedy and kinship.
- **Already locked:** he tells Sanguinarymon that one good battle doesn't complete a redemption.

### Ryo
- **Theme:** the one who never fully came home.
- **Proposed role:** Ryo spent much of the thirteen years crossing in and out of the Digital World. He's the only Tamer who knows what it looks like *now*, which makes him the guide for any Digital World arc.
- **Cyberdramon** hunted "the ultimate enemy" by scent during the D-Reaper crisis. Now that instinct fires again, because the prison is the D-Reaper's leftovers. **Cyberdramon is the early-warning system** before anyone understands the signal.
- **Cyberdramon vs the D-X:** a warrior who *chose* battle facing weapons who were never given a choice. ChaosGallantmon especially.
- **Canon continuity:** Ryo and Rika's teasing banter continues, only now they're adults and it's mutual respect with the same insults.

## Tier 2

### Kazu and Guardromon
- **Theme:** purpose after the mission. Kazu peaked at twelve. He's spent thirteen years telling people he used to be a Tamer and nobody believing him.
- **The hook: Guardromon and Anglemon.** Guardromon is a polite machine Digimon. Anglemon is a terrifying machine Digimon with no idea how to talk to people. **Guardromon befriends him and teaches him how to greet travelers.** That's how the "PASSAGE. AUTHORIZED." gag gets its heart.
- **Kazu sees himself in Anglemon:** the grunt, the sidekick, the one nobody remembers. His spotlight is realizing that being "secondary" never meant being nothing. (Anglemon, the grunt nobody remembers except his creator, gets a grunt nobody takes seriously as his first friend.)

### Kenta and MarineAngemon
- **Theme:** the scared one who shows up anyway.
- **Plot function:** MarineAngemon is a **Mega-level healer**. He's the team medic, and he helps keep Wulvermon's fragments stable during the reconstruction.
- **The hook: Kenta mentors Naoki.** Two anxious straight men. Kenta: "I've been the scared one for thirteen years. I still came."
- **Bonus:** MarineAngemon is a tiny Mega, which makes him another answer to Renamon's "what does the Mega state feel like?" question.

### Suzie and Lopmon
- **Theme:** being protected vs being trusted.
- **Suzie** is around nineteen and wants to be taken seriously. Henry's surveillance problem hits hardest with his own sister. She's the one who tells him he's turning into Yamaki before Terriermon has to.
- **Lopmon** was once Antylamon, a Deva who served a higher authority's orders. **She knows what it's like to obey a mission and then walk away from it.** That makes her a bridge to ChaosGallantmon and Anglemon.
- **Possible pairing:** Suzie and Mao as friends. The little sister and the street kid.

### Calumon
- **Theme:** growth as something the prison can't understand.
- **Plot function:** the prison treats uncontrolled evolution as D-Reaper-like, and **Calumon is the source of evolution.** That makes him a prime target, just like the D-Reaper hunted the Catalyst. A mid-story kidnapping or hunt fits naturally.
- **Already locked:** the Catalyst stops the prison from stripping evolutions in the finale.

## Tier 3

- **Yamaki:** now the head of Hypnos. He sees Henry becoming who he used to be and warns him, in as few words as possible. Gives the Tamers the final choice.
- **Riley and Tally:** Hypnos operators. Background support, small human moments.
- **Janyu and the Monster Makers:** they *created* Digimon. Perfect for the creation theme: a conversation between Janyu and Takato about making something that becomes its own person. They also investigate who made the T-X and D-X, and admit they may never know.
- **Kai:** Takato's cousin, an optional cameo.
- **Takato's parents:** bakery scenes, older, accepting Guilmon without question.
