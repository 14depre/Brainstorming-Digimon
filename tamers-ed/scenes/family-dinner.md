# Scene Draft: The Nonaka Family Dinner

> **Non-canon scene draft.** A comedy/breather scene set after the sisters reconcile with Renamon. Written in the transcript style of the English dub. Placement in the season is open.

---

*The Nonaka house. Evening. The table is set for six. Rika sits with her arms crossed. Renamon stands beside her.*

**Rika:** Why are there six plates?

**Seiko:** Because six people are coming.

**Rika:** Four. Renamon, two guests, and me. You and Mom make six. Fine. Forget it.

**Rumiko:** *(from the hallway)* Rika honey, is my hair okay? I want to make a good impression!

**Rika:** They're Digimon, Mom. They don't care about your hair.

**Renamon:** Tsukikagemon will notice it.

**Rika:** ...Great.

*The doorbell rings. Seiko checks the clock.*

**Seiko:** Seven o'clock exactly.

*Rika opens the door. Tsukikagemon stands outside, perfectly still, holding a neatly wrapped box with both hands. She bows.*

**Tsukikagemon:** Good evening. Thank you for the invitation. I was told it is customary to bring something when entering a family's home.

**Rika:** Uh. Yeah. Thanks.

**Tsukikagemon:** Is it also customary to remove one's footwear? I have no footwear. I have claws. I have filed them.

**Rika:** ...You filed your claws.

**Tsukikagemon:** For the floors.

*A loud crash comes from the garden. Something lands in the koi pond.*

**Nichirinmon:** *(off-screen)* I'M HERE!

**Renamon:** She came over the wall.

**Tsukikagemon:** I told her to use the door.

**Nichirinmon:** *(bursting in, dripping, solar halo steaming)* The door was for YOU. I'm family. Family uses the shortcut!

*She immediately crosses the room, lifts Renamon off her feet, and hugs her.*

**Nichirinmon:** Little sister!

**Renamon:** Put me down.

**Nichirinmon:** No.

**Renamon:** Rika.

**Rika:** You're on your own.

---

*Later. Everyone is seated. Tsukikagemon sits upright with perfect posture. Nichirinmon sits sideways in her chair. Renamon sits between them with the expression of a hostage.*

**Tsukikagemon:** *(to Seiko)* This tea is excellent. May I ask where the leaves are grown?

**Seiko:** Shizuoka. I've bought them from the same shop for thirty years.

**Tsukikagemon:** Thirty years of the same choice. I respect that very much.

**Seiko:** Thank you, dear.

**Tsukikagemon:** And this room. The scroll on the wall. Was it chosen for the season, or for meaning?

**Seiko:** Both, if you're lucky.

**Tsukikagemon:** *(quietly, to herself)* ...Both.

**Rika:** *(whispering to Renamon)* Is she always like this?

**Renamon:** She has been asking me questions about this house for a week.

**Rika:** She's been studying for dinner?

**Renamon:** She made notes.

**Nichirinmon:** *(mouth full)* This is GREAT! What is this?

**Rumiko:** Oh, that's just karaage!

**Nichirinmon:** Just karaage. JUST karaage! Tsukikagemon, write this down!

**Tsukikagemon:** I already have.

---

**Rumiko:** So! I have to ask. You two are girls, right?

*Renamon sets down her cup.*

**Renamon:** Actually, Digimon aren't divided into genders.

**Nichirinmon:** We're SISTERS. Obviously.

**Renamon:** That isn't what I—

**Nichirinmon:** Obviously.

**Renamon:** ...

**Tsukikagemon:** *(sipping tea)* She has decided.

**Seiko:** *(nodding)* That settles it, then.

**Renamon:** *(closing her eyes)* It does not settle anything.

**Rika:** It kind of does.

---

**Rumiko:** Oh, but sweethearts, you can't possibly eat properly with those masks on. Don't be shy! You're among family!

*Tsukikagemon and Nichirinmon glance at each other.*

**Tsukikagemon:** If it is the custom.

*They remove their masks.*

*Silence.*

*Rumiko drops her chopsticks.*

**Rumiko:** Oh.

**Rumiko:** Oh my GOSH.

**Rumiko:** EXCUSE ME??? HELLO??? THEY'RE SO BEAUTIFUL!

**Rika:** Mom.

**Rumiko:** Rika, LOOK at them! Look at the CHEEKBONES! The EYES! Mother, are you seeing this?!

**Seiko:** I'm seeing it.

**Rumiko:** *(already pulling out her phone)* I have a photographer friend. Do you two do modeling? Have you ever considered modeling? The moon one, with the crescent on her forehead, oh, I'm going to cry!

**Tsukikagemon:** *(very still)* I... was not prepared for this question.

**Nichirinmon:** *(leaning back, extremely smug)* I've considered it now.

**Rumiko:** You could do a SUN AND MOON spread! Twins! Well, not twins! Whatever you are!

**Renamon:** Sisters. Apparently.

**Rumiko:** And Renamon in the middle! The three of you! Oh, that's a cover!

**Renamon:** No.

**Nichirinmon:** Yes.

**Tsukikagemon:** *(after a pause, very small)* ...Perhaps one photograph.

**Renamon:** *(turning slowly to stare at her)* You too?

**Tsukikagemon:** She complimented the crescent.

---

**Nichirinmon:** Oh! Oh, Renamon! I just had the best idea!

**Renamon:** No.

**Nichirinmon:** You don't know what it is!

**Renamon:** I know that it is Taomon.

**Nichirinmon:** We need MATCHING HEIGHTS for the photo! Tsukikagemon, back me up!

**Tsukikagemon:** It would be more balanced.

**Renamon:** You have already tried to change my body once.

*The table goes quiet. Nichirinmon's halo dims a little. Tsukikagemon lowers her cup.*

**Nichirinmon:** ...Yeah. We did.

**Tsukikagemon:** We are sorry.

*A beat. Rika watches Renamon, not saying anything.*

**Renamon:** *(picking her cup back up)* I'll consider it for New Year's.

**Nichirinmon:** *(slamming both hands on the table)* YES!

**Seiko:** The table, dear.

**Nichirinmon:** Sorry, Grandma!

**Rika:** *(choking on her tea)* GRANDMA?

**Seiko:** *(completely unbothered)* I'll allow it.

---

*The doorbell rings. Everyone turns.*

**Rika:** We're not expecting anyone else.

*Seiko glances at the sixth plate. Rika follows her gaze. For a moment her face goes still, the old look, the one she used to wear waiting by the door.*

*Seiko opens the door.*

**Guilmon:** Hi! I brought bread!

**Takato:** *(out of breath, behind him)* I'm so sorry, Mrs. Hata, he heard "family dinner" and he just ran—

**Guilmon:** Renamon has sisters now! That means more guests! More guests means more bread!

**Rika:** *(the stillness leaving her face; she exhales something that's almost a laugh)* ...Of course.

**Seiko:** Come in, both of you. There's a plate.

**Takato:** There's... a plate? You set an extra—

**Seiko:** There's always a plate.

*Guilmon trots in, sees Tsukikagemon and Nichirinmon without their masks, and stops.*

**Guilmon:** Ohhh. Renamon, your sisters are SO PRETTY!

**Rumiko:** THANK YOU! FINALLY someone with TASTE!

**Nichirinmon:** I like this one. He can stay.

**Tsukikagemon:** He was in the photograph I am planning. Now he is in the planning.

**Renamon:** *(to Rika, flatly)* This is your fault.

**Rika:** How is this my fault?

**Renamon:** You opened the door.

*Rika looks around the table: her mother crying over cheekbones, her grandmother pouring tea for a Demon-sized appetite, Takato apologizing to a koi pond, Guilmon handing bread to a sun goddess, and Renamon pinned between two sisters who aren't letting go.*

**Rika:** *(quietly, mostly to herself)* Yeah. I did.

*She takes a piece of Guilmon's bread.*

**[END SCENE]**
