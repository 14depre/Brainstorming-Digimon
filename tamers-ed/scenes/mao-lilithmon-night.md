# Scene Draft: Two in the Morning

> **Non-canon scene draft.** Mao and Lilithmon at home, early in the story, before Mao fully trusts the Tamers. Written in the transcript style of the English dub.
>
> **Voice notes:** Mao is loud, physical and blunt, closer to Ryuko Matoi than to a quiet hacker. She yells first and explains never. Lilithmon is regal and long-winded when annoyed. No clean comebacks or mic-drop lines; arguments trail off, get interrupted, or end in actions.

---

*A cramped one-room apartment above a closed noodle shop. Cables everywhere. A futon nobody has used tonight. Mao sits cross-legged on the floor with the cracked black-and-gold D-Arc opened up in front of her, a soldering iron in one hand and a stolen circuit board in her teeth.*

*Lilithmon fills most of the room. She lounges across the only chair, which is too small for her, wings folded awkwardly against the ceiling.*

**Lilithmon:** It is two in the morning.

**Mao:** *(through the circuit board)* Mm-hm.

**Lilithmon:** You told me "five more minutes" at eleven.

**Mao:** *(spitting the board into her palm)* Yeah, and then the signal spiked again and the whole left side of the casing started buzzing, so I'm FIXING it, so sit there and be a chair or whatever.

**Lilithmon:** I am not a chair.

**Mao:** You're IN a chair. It's basically the same thing right now.

*Lilithmon shifts. The chair creaks dangerously.*

**Lilithmon:** That Hypnos boy. The one with the hair.

**Mao:** They ALL have hair.

**Lilithmon:** The tired one. With the ponytail. He looked at this device like it was a miracle, and you looked at him like he was going to steal it.

**Mao:** Because he WAS gonna steal it! That's what they do! You let a guy like that near your stuff and suddenly it's "for safety" and "for research," and then you never see it again and you're standing in a parking lot with nothing!

**Lilithmon:** He asked how you kept it running.

**Mao:** And I told him!

**Lilithmon:** You told him you bought the parts from an electronics store in Akihabara.

**Mao:** ...Some of them were from Akihabara.

**Lilithmon:** You stole them from an electronics store in Akihabara.

**Mao:** I didn't say I BOUGHT them in the—okay, I said that, but it was a—shut up, I'm working.

*She bends back over the D-Arc. The soldering iron hisses. For a while, the only sound is the refrigerator humming and the buzz of the device.*

*Then the cracks in the casing flare gold.*

*Lilithmon goes rigid. Her claws dig into the arms of the chair. Something in her shoulder flickers, the outline of her body briefly breaking into pixels before snapping back.*

**Mao:** *(dropping the iron)* OI! OI, HEY! Look at me! LOOK AT ME!

*She's already on her feet, already across the room, both hands on Lilithmon's face.*

**Lilithmon:** *(through her teeth)* I'm fine.

**Mao:** You're NOT fine, you're doing the glitchy thing! Don't do the glitchy thing! I said DON'T—hold still—

*She grabs the D-Arc off the floor, yanks a cable loose, and jams it into a different port. The gold light in the cracks dims. Lilithmon's outline steadies. Mao doesn't let go of her face.*

**Mao:** Is it better? Tell me if it's better. Don't do the proud thing, just TELL me.

**Lilithmon:** ...It's better.

**Mao:** Okay. Okay.

*She doesn't move. Her hands are shaking. She finally notices and shoves them into her hoodie pockets.*

**Mao:** That's the third time this week.

**Lilithmon:** The second.

**Mao:** It was THREE. Tuesday on the train counts. You think I didn't see you holding the pole like you were gonna rip it out of the ceiling?

**Lilithmon:** I was holding the pole because the train was moving.

**Mao:** You're a DEMON LORD, you don't need a pole!

*Lilithmon doesn't answer. Mao drops onto the floor at the foot of the chair, back against it, glaring at the D-Arc like it personally insulted her.*

**Mao:** It's that stupid signal. The underground one. Every time it gets louder, your anchor goes nuts, and I can't patch it fast enough because I don't know what it IS, and the only people who might know are a bunch of government nerds who want to put you in a box.

**Lilithmon:** Then let them help.

**Mao:** *(twisting around to stare up at her)* Excuse me?

**Lilithmon:** You heard me.

**Mao:** YOU'RE telling me to trust Hypnos? You? You made a whole security team lie down on the floor last week!

**Lilithmon:** They were in the way.

**Mao:** You can't just—that's not—ugh!

*She turns back around and pulls her knees up to her chest.*

**Mao:** I don't need them. We've been fine for thirteen years. Just us. I've always fixed it. I'll fix it this time too.

*Lilithmon looks down at the top of Mao's head for a long moment.*

**Lilithmon:** You were ten.

**Mao:** So?

**Lilithmon:** When it first broke. You were ten, and you had a screwdriver from a convenience store, and you sat on a wet sidewalk in Shinjuku for two days with your hands inside my data because you didn't know what else to do. I remember thinking that you were going to die of a cold before I died of anything else.

**Mao:** I didn't, though.

**Lilithmon:** No. You didn't.

*Pause.*

**Mao:** *(quieter)* Why didn't you go?

**Lilithmon:** Go where?

**Mao:** Back. When everyone got pulled back. All the other Digimon went. You could've gone. You'd have been fine over there, probably. Instead of falling apart on a sidewalk with some street kid poking at you.

**Lilithmon:** I am a Demon Lord. I don't go anywhere I'm told to go.

**Mao:** That's what you always say.

**Lilithmon:** Because it's true.

**Mao:** Yeah, but is that the WHOLE—

**Lilithmon:** Your solder is burning the floor.

**Mao:** *(spinning around)* AW, CRAP—

*She lunges for the soldering iron, which has been scorching a black mark into the floorboards this entire time. She stomps on the smoking spot, then blows on it, then pats it, then gives up.*

**Mao:** Landlord's gonna kill me.

**Lilithmon:** The landlord thinks you live alone with a very large dog.

**Mao:** He's never gonna buy that you're a dog.

**Lilithmon:** He has never seen me. He has heard me. He believes the dog is unwell.

*Mao snorts. Then she laughs, actually laughs, and has to put her forehead down on her knees.*

*She picks the D-Arc back up, checks the readout, and sets it gently on the floor between them.*

**Mao:** It's holding. For now.

**Lilithmon:** Then sleep.

**Mao:** In a minute.

**Lilithmon:** You said that at eleven.

**Mao:** I'm just gonna close my eyes. I'm not sleeping. I'm watching the readout with my eyes closed.

**Lilithmon:** That isn't possible.

**Mao:** It's possible if you're good at it.

*She tips sideways against the chair. Her hand stays on the D-Arc. Within a minute, her breathing slows.*

*Lilithmon looks at her for a long time.*

**Lilithmon:** *(very quietly)* ...You were ten. And you wouldn't let go.

*She doesn't finish the thought.*

*Carefully, more carefully than anything else she has done tonight, she rises from the chair, which groans in relief. She scoops Mao up, hoodie and all, along with the D-Arc still clutched in her hand, and carries her the three steps to the futon.*

**Lilithmon:** *(laying her down, muttering)* Every night. Every single night, on the floor, like a stray. I am a member of the Seven Great Demon Lords. I have leveled cities. And I am putting a child to bed.

*Mao mumbles something in her sleep and grabs a fistful of Lilithmon's sleeve.*

*Lilithmon sighs, long and theatrical, to no audience at all. Then she sits down on the floor beside the futon and stays there, watching the gold light pulse in the cracks of the D-Arc, until the window turns grey.*

**[END SCENE]**
