# Lyrics Draft: Tasogaremon

> **Non-canon draft. Not in the bible until approved.** Original lyrics for Tasogaremon's songs. Rika's sunset song is not rewritten here; it stays the real song.

---

## 1. "Taso Kare" (Who Is That Over There?)

*The song the music box plays. Tasogaremon hums it, and occasionally sings it softly. It is named after the phrase that "tasogare" (twilight) comes from.*

> Who is that over there,
> at the end of the evening street?
> A shirt the color of the sky,
> a shadow at his feet.
>
> Leave the porch light burning,
> leave the gate undone.
> He's only just around the bend.
> He's walking toward the sun.
>
> Count the cars, count the crows,
> count until the light.
> I'll sit with you, I'll sit with you,
> I'll sit with you all night.
>
> Who is that over there?
> Is he coming home to—

*The last line never ends. That cut is the unresolved four-note phrase.*

### Music box decay (proposed)
Each time he appears, the music box plays one line less:

| Appearance | What plays |
|---|---|
| First | The full song, up to "coming home to—" |
| Middle | Only verse 1 |
| Late | Only "Who is that over there?" |
| Final | "Who is—" and then the box runs out |

## 2. "Yūyake Koyake," Tasogaremon's version

*He sings along with the 5 p.m. town chime, in English, under his breath. The first lines follow the original children's song (public domain, 1919). The last line belongs to him.*

> Evening glow, the day is done,
> the temple bell rings down the hill.
> Hold hands, everyone, let's all go home,
> go home together with the crows.
>
> *(spoken)* Everyone but you, Miss Nonaka. You'll wait.

---

## 3. "Hotaru no Hikari": no lyrics

*It is never sung. It plays as a melody only, the way shops play it at closing time. The only time it plays all the way through is when he fades.*
