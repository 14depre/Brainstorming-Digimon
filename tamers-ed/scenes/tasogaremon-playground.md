# Scene Draft: The Queen of the Sandbox

> **Non-canon scene draft.** Rika, alone, on a good day. Demonstrates Tasogaremon's worst-moment pattern: it shows up when she isn't waiting for anything. Written in the transcript style of the English dub. Placement in the season is open.

---

*A residential street in Shinjuku. Late summer, early evening. The sky is going orange. Rika, twenty-three, walks home from the convenience store with a plastic bag swinging from one hand: two bottles of tea and a bag of the cheap rice crackers Seiko pretends she doesn't like.*

*She passes a small playground. Four kids, maybe eight years old, are crouched around a bench, slapping Digimon cards down on it.*

**Kid 1:** I play Renamon! Diamond Storm! You're DELETED!

**Kid 2:** You can't play Renamon on my turn, that's cheating!

**Kid 1:** The Digimon Queen doesn't need turns!

**Kid 3:** You're not the Digimon Queen, you're Haruka.

**Kid 1:** *(standing on the bench, hands on hips)* Do I LOOK like I need rescuing?

*Rika stops walking.*

*She stands at the fence for a second, bag hanging from her fingers. Something happens to her face that she would deny under oath.*

**Kid 2:** That's not even a real line!

**Kid 1:** It is so! My cousin saw it!

**Kid 4:** *(not looking up from her cards)* The Digimon Queen had purple hair.

**Kid 1:** She had RED hair, she was like a fire, she was so cool—

**Kid 4:** Purple.

*Rika opens her mouth. Closes it. Decides it's not worth it. Keeps walking.*

*Behind her, the argument continues.*

**Kid 1:** *(yelling)* Okay, FINE, I'm the Digimon Queen with PURPLE hair, and I'm STILL deleting you!

*Rika laughs out loud, once, alone on the sidewalk. She shakes her head. She's still smiling when she reaches the corner.*

*A voice calls from down the block: a man in a work shirt, waving.*

**Father:** Haruka! Dinner!

**Kid 1:** *(off-screen)* DAD! Dad, I was the Digimon Queen!

**Father:** *(off-screen, laughing)* Of course you were. Come on, your mom's waiting.

*Rika doesn't turn around. She hears small feet running, a happy shriek as someone gets lifted off the ground. The smile is still there. She thinks, briefly, that it's a nice evening.*

*She waits at the crosswalk for the light to change.*

*The light is red for a long time.*

*Her shadow stretches long across the street in front of her. Next to it, another shadow begins, far too long and far too thin, from someone standing directly behind her left shoulder.*

**Tasogaremon:** *(a whisper, very close, very polite)* What a lovely evening, Miss Nonaka.

*Rika goes completely still. The plastic bag stops swinging.*

**Tasogaremon:** You were smiling. I'm sorry to interrupt. It's only that I so rarely see it.

*She doesn't turn around. Across the street, at the far end of the next block, a man in a short-sleeved shirt with a briefcase is walking away from her, toward the sun.*

**Tasogaremon:** He used to lift you like that. Do you remember? No. I suppose that's why his face is so difficult.

**Rika:** *(barely)* Get away from me.

**Tasogaremon:** Of course. I'll just keep you company until the light changes.

*The light is still red. The man with the briefcase is still walking, getting smaller, never getting farther away.*

*Rika's hand tightens on the bag until the plastic squeaks. She doesn't look at the man. She looks at the crosswalk signal and counts, under her breath, the way she used to count in the dark.*

*Green.*

*She steps off the curb and walks. She doesn't run.*

**Tasogaremon:** *(behind her, unhurried)* Tomorrow, then. Same hour. Please give my regards to your grandmother.

*When she reaches the other side, she turns around.*

*The crosswalk is empty. Her shadow is the only one on the street. The man with the briefcase is gone. From two blocks away, faintly, she can still hear Haruka shouting that the Digimon Queen has purple hair.*

*Rika stands there for a long moment. Then she pulls out her phone, opens it, and types with her thumb.*

**Rika:** *(text message)* where are you

*An answer comes almost instantly, before she has even locked the screen. Renamon drops onto the wall beside her, silent, already looking up and down the street.*

**Renamon:** Here.

**Rika:** I know. *(beat)* Walk with me.

*Renamon falls in at her side. Rika doesn't explain, and Renamon doesn't ask. Halfway home, Rika hands her the bag of rice crackers to carry, and that's the only thing either of them says about it.*

**[END SCENE]**
