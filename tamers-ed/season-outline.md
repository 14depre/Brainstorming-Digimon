# Digimon Tamers: E.D. — Season Outline (Draft 1)

> **First-pass structure.** Built from the master narrative's material, re-paced with room to breathe. Episode titles and beats are proposals to react to, cut, merge or move. Nothing here is locked.

## Format

- **Length:** 51 episodes, matching the original Tamers (placeholder until confirmed)
- **Arcs:** 5
- **Tags:** 🌿 = breather / character episode · 🧬 = DNA debut · ⚔️ = major battle · 📜 = lore reveal

| Arc | Episodes | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Reunion | 1–9 | Coming home, meeting the new Tamers, Mao, Chaosmon's first appearance |
| 2. Fusion Killer | 10–19 | Support cast returns, first DNA forms, Chaosmon as the early threat, the prison discovered |
| 3. Awakening | 20–30 | ChaosGallantmon, Sanguinarymon, the sisters, the prison expands |
| 4. Brother Conflict | 31–42 | Wulvermon's death and return, Sanguinarymon and ChaosGallantmon turn |
| 5. Final Battle | 43–51 | The seal over Shinjuku, the descent, the shutdown, the epilogue |

## Arc 1: Reunion (Episodes 1–9)

**Goal:** Let the reunions breathe. Establish the adult cast's normal lives before anything gets big.

1. **Thirteen Years** 🌿 — A day in each life. Takato at the bakery with notebooks he never opens. Henry at Hypnos chasing false alarms. Rika training alone and ignoring a text from her father. At night, all three D-Arcs light up at once.
2. **The Tunnel** ⚔️ — The trio meets at Guilmon's old hiding place. Rogue Digimon attack the forming portal. Naoki and an un-partnered Salvodramon interrupt. Guilmon, Terriermon and Renamon come through. *"Was thirteen years long enough?"*
3. **Home** 🌿 — No fights. The bakery dinner, Takato checking the kitchen. Terriermon closing Henry's laptop. Rumiko lasting a few seconds. Rika moving her bedding to the window.
4. **There Was No Plan** — Naoki's episode. His ordinary life, the weeks he's been hiding Salvodramon, and why he never told anyone. A hunter goes for Salvodramon's core; Naoki steps in and gets his D-Arc. Raygundramon's first appearance.
5. **The Odds Became Offensive** — Kei's episode. The underground card scene, the cards that rewrite themselves, and the rumors about the "Digimon Queen" who quit. He finds Wulvermon holding the corridor and gets his D-Arc.
6. **Bread for Strangers** 🌿 — Hypnos intake. Guilmon's bread diplomacy and Salvodramon's ammunition-shaped bread. Naoki and Kei's first real conversation as total opposites. Kei realizes who Rika is.
7. **The Girl Who Steals** — Mao hacks Hypnos. Rika blocks the exit. Lilithmon cracks the D-Arc open and the whole room stands down.
8. **Hand-Installed** 🌿 — Henry asks how she kept the D-Arc alive and Mao assumes it's an accusation. Renamon spots the hand-soldered circuits. The deal, and the stolen second copy. Back home: the trench-coat disguise, the arguing, Mao falling asleep somewhere stupid.
9. **The Cage Is Hungry** ⚔️ — The hunters turn out to be bribed by a buried signal. A strange asymmetrical Digimon tears through them *and* the Tamers: **Chaosmon**. He warns that human–Digimon links are "feeding the cage" and leaves. Jeri sees him from a distance and freezes.

## Arc 2: Fusion Killer (Episodes 10–19)

**Goal:** Chaosmon gives the first half a face. DNA Digivolution gets introduced one fusion at a time. The prison stays mostly hidden.

10. **Guardromon Found a Relay** 🌿 — Kazu and Guardromon, Kenta and MarineAngemon. A comedy episode about 25-year-olds reuniting with their childhood partners.
11. **Lopmon's Gate** 🌿 — Henry stabilizes a gate for Suzie and Lopmon and can't stop over-engineering her safety. Ryo and Cyberdramon arrive from elsewhere.
12. **No Single Apology** 🌿 — Impmon goes to Ai and Mako first. Then Jeri. Neither tries to fix thirteen years in one sentence.
13. **The Card He Didn't Play** ⚔️ — Chaosmon returns. Takato holds back a power card and Guilmon gets hit waiting for it. *"You drew my picture. Everything after belonged to both of us."*
14. **Abyss** 🧬⚔️ — Chaosmon traps Guilmon and Salvodramon in a collapsing transit layer. **Abyssdramon** forms feral and nameless until Takato and Naoki name it, then rescues the trapped Digimon. Chaosmon watches the fusion and something in him breaks: he becomes the **Fusion Killer**.
15. **Calumon's Shelters** 🌿 — Hypnos opens Digimon shelters. Calumon triggers accidental evolutions. Kei hustles shelter Digimon at cards. Henry starts sounding like Yamaki and Yamaki notices.
16. **Tango de Muerte** 🧬 — The data-exchange raid. **Tangomon** fails because Rika treats Salvodramon like borrowed equipment, and Renamon sides with Naoki. The second attempt works.
17. **Not Leomon** ⚔️ — Chaosmon hunts Tangomon. Jeri recognizes BanchoLeomon's data and refuses to call him Leomon. He retreats, shaken, and lets slip that he can feel something *under* the Digital World forcing data together.
18. **Defective Unit** 📜 — The signal names Wulvermon "defective." Salvodramon gets headaches and his weapons fire on their own. Mao and Rika investigate the ID marks together. *"Concealing a departure from someone waiting for you is cowardly."*
19. **Empty Impressions** 📜⚔️ — Following Chaosmon's hint, the Tamers descend beneath the network. Thousands of empty Digimon-shaped hollows. **Anglemon** wakes and attacks, and before they escape he activates two D-X chambers. Final shot: a knight's silhouette in the dark.

## Arc 3: Awakening (Episodes 20–30)

**Goal:** Introduce the D-X one at a time. The sisters arrive. Reveals come from characters with biases, not files.

20. **Unauthorized Copy** ⚔️ — ChaosGallantmon vs Gallantmon. He hears two voices and calls it instability. The prison orders him to withdraw with no winner.
21. **Two Voices** 🌿 — Takato and Guilmon talk about what Gallantmon actually is. Chaosmon reappears and, for once, doesn't attack, because a second enemy just proved his warning right.
22. **Raydramon** ⚔️ — Sanguinarymon wakes, ignores everyone else and hunts Salvodramon. He dismantles every weapon and calls him a name he doesn't know.
23. **Recognizably Yours** 📜🌿 — Mao pulls the Raydramon file from the attack data. Salvodramon stops joking. Naoki: *"They chose your birth, not your choices."* The first real Naoki and Kei duo moments.
24. **Lunar Interception** 🧬 — Naoki and Kei can't sync. Salvodramon overcommits and Wulvermon over-protects. **Moondramon** forms the moment they stop fighting each other's style.
25. **Sun and Moon** ⚔️ — Tsukikagemon and Nichirinmon beat Gallantmon *and* MegaGargomon, then stop dead when Renamon appears. *"Does Renamon have sisters?"* Guilmon immediately wants to invite them to the bakery.
26. **The Version She Remembers** 📜 — Tsukikagemon visits Renamon alone at night and tells her the story of Taikyokumon her way: as an injury that must be healed. The audience learns the lore through someone who has it wrong.
27. **Vermilion Ward** 🧬 — An attack through Rika's D-Arc hits her home with Seiko and Rumiko inside. **Komainudramon** wins by standing still, which is the hardest thing Rika has ever done.
28. **Absence and Abandonment** 🌿 — Rika tries to plan Renamon away from her sisters. Renamon: *"Being afraid doesn't give you the right to decide in advance."* Wulvermon starts quietly showing up. Kei notices and Rika glares him into silence.
29. **Heavy Things Fall Upward** 🧬 — Takato opens the sketchbook for the first time in years. **Jabberwockmon** comes out of it to stop a mobile gateway headed for central Shinjuku.
30. **Parhelion, Failed** 📜 — Parhelimon falls apart chasing the sisters because both partners are using protection to dodge Renamon's choice. Henry and Mao figure out that the prison is a procedure with no prisoner. Chaosmon admits his theory was only half right.

## Arc 4: Brother Conflict (Episodes 31–42)

**Goal:** The missing arc. Everything the draft skipped happens here, with grief given real space.

31. **Friday** 🌿 — Rika and Mao hang out like normal people for once. Lilithmon is jealous of Mao's new friend. Rika's father cancels another meeting and she pretends not to care.
32. **The Harlequin's Trick** 🧬 — The prison sends its mechanisms (Klaxonmon, Hushmon). **Harequinmon** debuts: Rika has to trust a plan only Henry fully understands.
33. **Deadstick Dive** 🧬 — Suturemon is sewing breaches shut with people still inside. **Barnstormon** chases it, with Henry and Naoki both terrified and pretending otherwise.
34. **The Place Has Already Failed** — Anglemon tries to resume the old sacrifice. The Tamers pull him out, and he begs to go back. Wulvermon tells him the truth. He retreats alone.
35. **Arsenal** ⚔️ — **ArsenalHydramon** first appears and beats Sanguinarymon with patterns no record predicted. Salvodramon acknowledges the theft and refuses to apologize for existing. Naoki's D-Arc absorbs what's left.
36. **Witness** 🌿 — Sanguinarymon awake inside the D-Arc, watching two people treat his data like a person.
37. **I** ⚔️ — The sisters force the Taikyokumon restoration. It speaks as "I" and starts absorbing Renamon. Wulvermon pulls her out and is deleted.
38. **Unrecoverable** 🌿 — The grief episode. Kei. Renamon. The sisters recover the real memory: Taikyokumon *chose* to end. Rika uses the loss to shut the sisters out for good.
39. **Last Trail** 🧬 — Takato and Kei refuse to call Wulvermon gone. **Cairndramon** debuts, walking into the prison archive and finding fragments in Vellumon's records.
40. **Caritas** 🧬 — Mao opens the system that saved Lilithmon thirteen years ago. **Caritasmon** debuts. With Henry and Kei, they rebuild Wulvermon around the bond. He recognizes Kei.
41. **A Malfunction** ⚔️ — ChaosGallantmon's second fight. He obeys an order that will delete him; Takato and Guilmon refuse to let him. Then comes the contradictory order, and he turns on the conduits.
42. **Not Brothers** ⚔️ — Sanguinarymon breaks out of the D-Arc and goes **EX**. Rika finally lets the sisters back in. The arc ends with a dark circle opening over Shinjuku.

## Arc 5: Final Battle (Episodes 43–51)

**Goal:** Pay off everything. Give each character one moment that's theirs.

43. **Harbor** 🧬⚔️ — Shinjuku is turning into archive. **Breakwatermon** shelters civilians and nearly overloads. Henry and Kei have to order the discharge.
44. **Several Hours** 🌿 — Yamaki's choice to sever the worlds. The night before the descent: each pair gets a quiet scene.
45. **Outer Routes** ⚔️ — The support cast holds the routes and Calumon holds the evolutions. Chaosmon chooses to guide them because Jeri won't decide for him.
46. **Passage** ⚔️ — Anglemon at the gate, the lure luring and his voice warning. Moondramon frees him. He opens the lock and gets pulled out.
47. **The Correct Apology** — The illusions. Rika's father apologizes perfectly, and that's how she knows it's fake. Henry and Terriermon break theirs with one forbidden button.
48. **The Line He Drew** — Takato's perfect childhood bakery. The illusion can't imagine an answer he never gave. He draws a line it didn't predict.
49. **The Archived Dead** ⚔️ — The dead T-X and D-X rise. ChaosGallantmon takes a hit for Gallantmon. Beelzemon and Sanguinarymon argue about redemption back to back.
50. **We** ⚔️ — The linked D-Arcs, the fusions in rotation, Taikyokumon answering "we," and the shutdown sequence entered piece by piece.
51. **Passage Authorized** 🌿 — The collapse, the stable gates and the epilogue: the drawing, the internship, Friday, the badly framed photo, the cuddle pile, and Anglemon at his gate scaring the first travelers.
