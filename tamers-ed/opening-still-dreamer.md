# Opening Theme: "STILL DREAMER!" (full version, draft 1)

> **Draft.** Original lyrics for the *Passage Authorized* opening. The vibe takes after the original *Tamers* opening and follows a similar song structure, but no lines are borrowed. The romaji and the Spanish are written separately (the Latin dub approach), so they match in feeling, not word for word. Hooks: **STILL DREAMER!** (romaji version, Engrish) and **¡Sigue soñando!** (Spanish version). The show title is deliberately not name-dropped.
>
> **TV size** = Intro, Verse 1 (first half), Pre-chorus 1, Chorus 1, Outro.

## Hidden meanings
- **The drawer:** the D-Arcs the Tamers kept for thirteen years.
- **Necktie, train, stranger in the window / "un extraño me miró y no era yo":** adult life. The face nobody recognizes echoes *taso kare* ("who is that over there?"), the phrase Tasogaremon's name comes from.
- **"When the sunset calls... I won't turn around" / "Si el ocaso me llama, no voltearé":** Tasogaremon and the end of Rika's arc. It reads as normal hype until the finale.
- **"I'm coming to get you" / "voy por ti":** the reunion with the partners.
- **"If you call me, I'll answer":** the Tamer and partner bond.
- **The bell (Pre-chorus 2):** the 5 p.m. chime, "Yūyake Koyake," whose lyrics mention a temple bell.
- **Bridge, "someone is waiting on the sunset road... but I know where home is":** Tasogaremon's lure fails. This is the song's version of Rika letting go.
